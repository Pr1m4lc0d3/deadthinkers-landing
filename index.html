Who is in the library

Plato · Hypatia · Zhuangzi · Ibn Sina · Murasaki Shikibu · Al‑Khwārizmī · Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz · Émilie du Châtelet · Michael Faraday · Ada Lovelace · Frederick Douglass · Marie Curie

The first three hundred are being chosen — balanced across centuries and traditions, so the shelves are not three hundred Western men. Their dates and birthplaces come from the public record rather than from a model, because a wrong birth date on a scholarly page costs everything else on it.

After that the library grows by being used. Summon a mind we don’t have yet, and the page you leave behind is waiting for whoever searches that name next. You would be writing the library.

Not a costume

The ruler who emerges from his tenure with clean hands and a ruined state has failed both his people and any serious standard of virtue. The question of whether his soul is intact is a question for his confessor. It is not a question for history.

Niccolò Machiavelli — unedited, from a real run of Deliberon’s persona engine. Dead Thinkers is built on that same engine.

And he knows nothing after 1527. Ask him about the Cold War and he cannot answer — he can only reason toward it from what he had, and be delighted or appalled by where it leads. That limit is not a flaw waiting to be patched. It is the most interesting thing in the room.
